# Product Requirements Document (PRD): CampusCycle

## 1. Problem Statement
- **The Problem:** Navigating across a large university campus like Babcock University between lectures, hostels, libraries, and administrative blocks is time-consuming and physically exhausting. On-campus tricycles are few in number, unreliable, and often unavailable during peak hours, 
- **Impact:** Thousands of undergraduate and postgraduate students, as well as campus staff, are affected daily—especially during morning lecture rushes (7:00 AM–10:00 AM) and late afternoon transitions (3:00 PM–6:00 PM), leading to frequent lateness to classes and wasted commute time.

## 2. Target Audience & Stakeholders
**Primary Users:**

- **Students:** Commuting between hostels, lecture halls, and study areas on a tight schedule.
- **Campus Staff & Lecturers:** Moving between faculty buildings and administrative offices.

**Secondary Stakeholders:**

- **Campus Security & Transport Unit:** Responsible for fleet safety, station monitoring, and preventing bike theft or unauthorized off-campus removal.
- **Maintenance Technicians:** Responsible for repairing damaged bicycles and rebalancing bikes across stations.
- **University Administration:** Funds and oversees campus mobility initiatives.

## 3.Current workarounds
- **How students cope today:*Students cope by going to class extra early so as to not be late for thier classes. Some students have the contact details of some tricycle drivers and can call them when they need to go to somewhere
