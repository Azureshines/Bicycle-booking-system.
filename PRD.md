#Product Requirements Document(PRD):Campus cycle 


##1. Problem Statement:
 Navigating across a large university campus like Babcock University between lectures, hostels, libraries, and administrative blocks is time-consuming and physically exhausting. On-campus tricycles are few in number, unreliable and often unavailable during peak hours, and existing campus bicycles are managed through disorganized manual logs, resulting in unaccounted bikes, frequent theft or hoarding, and riders arriving at docking stations only to find zero working bicycles available. Thousands of undergraduate and postgraduate students, as well as campus staff, are affected daily—especially during morning lecture rushes (7:00 AM–10:00 AM) and late afternoon transitions (3:00 PM–6:00 PM), leading to frequent lateness to classes and wasted commute time.

##2.Target Audience & Stakeholders
**Primary Users**:

***Students***: Commuting between hostels, lecture halls, and study areas on a tight schedule.
***Campus Staff & Lecturers***: Moving between faculty buildings and administrative offices.
**Secondary Stakeholders**:
***Campus Security & Transport Unit***: Responsible for fleet safety, station monitoring, and preventing bike theft or unauthorized off-campus removal.
***Maintenance Technicians***: Responsible for repairing damaged bicycles and rebalancing bikes across stations.
***University Administration***: Funds and oversees campus mobility initiatives.
